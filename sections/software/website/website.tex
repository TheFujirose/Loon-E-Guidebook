\subsection{Website}
\label{sec:website}
HumberASV's website is a component for the RoboBoat competition \autocite{ROBOBOAT}.
It details our team, our ASV, and our software, and provides a way for the public to learn about our project and follow our progress. 
The website is built using modern web technologies (Vite and React) and is designed to be responsive and user-friendly. 
It includes sections on our team members, our ASV's design and capabilities, and technical report.
